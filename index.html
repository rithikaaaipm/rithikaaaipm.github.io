Background

Customer-first delivery, backed by data and AI fluency.

My career has run through technical support, customer success, and service delivery in IoT, consumer telecom, and B2B SaaS/manufacturing-tech — starting on Level 1/2 support and progressing to Team Leader, managing 15+ direct reports. I hold an MSc in Business Analytics from the University of Exeter and a BEng in Electrical & Electronics Engineering, which means I can sit with engineers, operations teams, and senior stakeholders in the same conversation.

At BT Group I was promoted from Customer Service Advisor to Team Leader within my first year. I coached and led a team of 15+ support staff, directed readiness for a roaming services rollout across a 15 million-customer base, and translated recurring complaint patterns into structured requirements for a new Complaints Management System — closing the loop with engineering using Jira and Confluence.

More recently, at a B2B SaaS startup digitising jewelry manufacturing shop floors (clients including Titan Engineering and Emerald Jewellers), I ran discovery and stakeholder interviews to convert pen-and-paper tracking into a real-time digital system — co-designing gamified, multilingual, face-scan features so the product would actually get adopted by non-literate and low-literacy factory workers.

Earlier, at McWane's smart water metering business, I owned end-to-end technical support and implementation for enterprise IoT clients, including a 70,000-unit rollout in Gujarat — one of the company's largest deployments — configuring meter-to-cloud data flows in SQL/MySQL and gathering client requirements that fed directly into the product roadmap.

I'm now pursuing Customer Success and Support Operations roles where I can bring that same empathetic, root-cause approach to client relationships — backed by program management, business analysis, and increasingly, AI-native product thinking.
